The Guidance Process

How Your Consultation Proceeds

Follow our systematic 6-step framework to describe your farming situation clearly before deciding on any inputs.

1

Provide Farmer & Farm Details

Share your location (state and district), land size, and contact details so recommendations align with your regional soil conditions and local climate.

2

Describe Crop & Condition

Identify your crop name, current growth stage (from sowing to flowering or grain filling), and soil type (or select "I don't know my soil type").

3

Share Problem or Outcome

Describe observed symptoms such as leaf discoloration, slow tillering, root health, or state your target yield and nutrient replenishment goals.

4

Select Consultation Mode

Choose between Online Consultation (₹2,499 per session) or an in-person Field Visit Consultation (₹5,999 per visit + separate travel & food expenses).

5

Choose Date & Time Slot

Pick an upcoming date and time window (Morning, Afternoon, or Evening) that fits your schedule without interrupting field operations.

6

Review & Receive Reference

Review all entered parameters, submit your request, and receive a dedicated advisory reference code (`KK-GUID-XXXXXX`) for your consultation record.
